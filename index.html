About Me

An engineer who likes to build things that work.

I started out as a petrochemical engineer in Ghana, designing process plants and teaching chemistry. I then moved to the United States for a Master's in Engineering Management. Along the way I worked in logistics, process engineering and field analytics, and the job that kept coming up was the same: take scattered data, find what matters and present it so people can make good decisions.

That's what led me to software. I'm currently in the University of Texas at Arlington AI-Centric Software Development bootcamp, studying front-end and back-end development, modern software engineering and cloud-ready development. I'm also pursuing a Doctor of Business Administration in Data Analytics and Business Intelligence.

My goal is to build software that pairs solid engineering thinking with clean, usable design, especially tools that help teams understand their data and run operations better.
